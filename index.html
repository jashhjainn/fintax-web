About Us

What is FinTax?

Fintax is an intelligent financial management platform designed to simplify GST compliance and financial tracking for businesses. Our platform provides advanced OCR technology to automate invoice processing.

How Fintax Works

1

Upload Invoice

Upload your GST invoices through our web interface or mobile camera. Fintax supports multiple formats including PDF, images, and scanned documents.

2

Smart Processing

Our advanced OCR technology extracts key information including GSTIN, invoice numbers, amounts, and tax details with high accuracy.

3

Data Validation

Intelligent validation checks ensure data accuracy and compliance with GST regulations, flagging any discrepancies for review.

4

Dashboard Analytics

View comprehensive financial insights including total sales, gst payable and reports.

5

Export & Compliance

Generate GST-compliant reports and export data in various formats for filing and auditing purposes.
